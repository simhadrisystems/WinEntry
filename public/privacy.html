Privacy Policy

WinEntry — Inventory Management App  |  Effective date: 16 April 2026  |  Last updated: 25 April 2026 (v2.2)

WinEntry ("the App") is an inventory management application designed for retail businesses that manage products as countable units (such as packaged or bottled items), across multiple brands and sizes. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By using WinEntry, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

WinEntry is developed and operated by Simhadri Systems (simhadrisystems@gmail.com). This is a privately operated business application — it is not a consumer data product and does not collect personal data beyond what is necessary for business operations.

2. Our Data Principle

Your inventory data is never transmitted to any cloud service without your knowledge. Cloud backup requires your explicit opt-in. Once activated, the app performs automatic background sync (every 6 hours) to keep your workspace up to date — you are informed of this at the point of activation. You can deactivate automatic sync and switch to manual-only sync at any time from Settings (see Section 5). The app does not run background data collection, analytics pipelines, or any form of tracking.

3. What Information Is Collected

3a. Information you provide

  • Business details (Settings → Business Info) — Stored locally on your device. Business name is the only required field; owner/contact name, phone number, and location are optional and can be left blank. When you tap "Save & Register" to request a cloud workspace, your business name and any optional fields you have filled in are shared with the app administrator solely for provisioning purposes.
  • Licence number and address — Stored locally on your device only. These fields are never transmitted to the administrator or any cloud service.
  • Inventory records — Daily stock entries, purchase records, day-end reconciliation figures. Stored locally in a database on your device. Cloud sync is optional and entirely user-initiated (see Section 5).

3b. Information obtained through Google Sign-In

WinEntry uses Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication) for user identity. When you sign in, the following information is obtained from your Google account:

3c. Sample / test data

The app includes an optional Import Test Data feature (Settings → Help & Support → Import Test Data) that downloads a pre-populated sample product list from the admin's standard product sheet. This is available only on fresh installs with no existing daily stock records, and is intended purely for exploration and initial setup:

  • Sample product codes, names, and quantities are reference values only.
  • You are expected to review and replace them with your actual business data before live use.
  • No personal data is uploaded during this operation — it is a one-way download.

3d. Technical data and error logs

  • Android version — recorded once in the administrator's user registry when you submit a workspace request. Used solely for support and compatibility purposes. Device model is not collected.
  • Error log — If the app encounters a technical error, a brief record (timestamp, screen name, and error detail) is written to a private file on your device. This file is accessible from the Settings screen — you can share it manually when reporting a problem, or clear it at any time. It is never transmitted automatically. No third-party crash analytics service is used.

5. How Your Data Is Used

You are always in control of your data and when it is synced.

Cloud backup in WinEntry is always optional and user-initiated. You must explicitly request a workspace from the admin and accept the consent terms before any inventory data leaves your device. Signing in with Google alone does not start any cloud sync. You can use the app indefinitely with local storage and Excel export only, without ever enabling cloud backup.

WinEntry does not sell your personal data to any third party, and does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the operation of the app. This applies to all data collected, including Google account information obtained via Google Sign-In.

  • Sync schedule — and how to control it — Once cloud backup is active, the app performs a background sync approximately every 6 hours using Android's WorkManager. You are informed of this at the point of consent. You can also trigger a manual sync at any time from the Sync button on the Home screen. To switch to manual-only, go to Settings → Drive Backup card → Deactivate (stops automatic sync; manual sync from the Home screen remains available). To re-enable automatic sync, reactivate the Drive Backup card.
  • Sign-in only — no automatic cloud access — Signing in with Google creates your identity in Firebase so the admin can identify and provision your workspace. It does not automatically connect you to any spreadsheet. No inventory data leaves your device at sign-in.
  • Administrator read access — Once cloud backup is active, the administrator has full read access to all records synced to your workspace spreadsheet. This is a deliberate feature of the admin-managed workspace model. If you do not want the administrator to see your data, do not enable cloud backup.
  • Administrator provisioning — Your email, display name, and any business registration details you provided (business name, and optionally owner name, phone, and location) are shared with the app administrator (simhadrisystems@gmail.com) solely for the purpose of setting up your cloud workspace.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party sharing — Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, or analytics purposes. Your inventory data and business information are never shared with or sold to any third party other than the services listed in Section 6.

7. Data Storage and Retention

  • On-device — Inventory data is stored in a local database on your Android device. It remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data. This data is not included in Android's automatic device backup — it stays on your device only.
  • Google Sheets — Your synced inventory records remain in your assigned spreadsheet in the administrator's Google Drive workspace. The administrator manages retention of the workspace spreadsheet. You can delete all your cloud sheet data yourself using the in-app account deletion feature (see Section 9), or contact us at simhadrisystems@gmail.com to request deletion without deleting your account.

    User responsibility for data backup: You are strongly advised to periodically export your data using the app's built-in Excel export feature as an independent local backup. The administrator will take reasonable care of the cloud workspace, but is not responsible for any data loss arising from Google service outages, spreadsheet deletion, sync failures, or other circumstances beyond direct control.
  • Firebase Firestore — Your user profile record (email, name, sheet ID) is retained for as long as your account is active. Accounts with no cloud sync activity for more than 3 months are considered inactive and will be scheduled for data purge. You will receive a 7-day advance email notification to your registered Google account address before any such purge is carried out, giving you the opportunity to cancel or reactivate. You may also request deletion at any time — see Section 9.

9. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Delete your account — in-app (immediate, no waiting)

If you have the app installed, you can delete your account and all associated cloud data yourself — no administrator involvement and no waiting period.

Path: Settings → Business Info → ⋮ (top-right menu) → Delete Account & Data

The following are permanently and immediately deleted. This cannot be undone.

  • Account registration — your name, phone number, business name, location, display name, and app version are blanked from the administrator's user registry records. Your email address and a deletion timestamp are retained in the registry row as an administrative audit trail (see note below).
  • Cloud inventory data — all daily stock records, purchase records, and day-end reconciliation records in your cloud workspace spreadsheet
  • Sign-in account — your email and business profile are permanently removed from Firebase Authentication and Firestore
Audit record retained — what and why:

When you delete your account, a minimal audit record is retained in an administrator-side log. This record contains only your email address, the date of deletion, and a deletion count. No inventory data, no name, no phone number, no business details are included.

This record is retained for security and fraud prevention purposes — specifically to detect and prevent abuse of the free workspace registration system (for example, repeatedly registering and deleting accounts to obtain resources). It is an administrative record — not a user data record — and is not subject to the account deletion flow. You may request removal of this audit record separately by contacting simhadrisystems@gmail.com.

Local device data: Inventory data stored on your device is not deleted automatically. After the cloud deletion completes, you will be asked separately whether you also want to clear it from the device. The choice is yours — local data is under your exclusive control.

Delete your account — web form (if you no longer have the app)

If you no longer have the app installed, submit a deletion request using the web form at:
https://sites.google.com/view/winentryapp/account-deletion

Requests submitted via the web form are processed by the administrator within 7 days. A confirmation email will be sent to the address you provide once deletion is complete.

Other rights

  • Access — You can access your synced inventory data through the app by performing a down-sync (Sync button → pulls latest cloud records into your local database) and then viewing it within the app, exporting it to Excel, or both. Direct access to the raw Google Spreadsheet in the administrator's workspace is not provided to end users.
  • Deactivate or control cloud backup — Go to Settings → Drive Backup card → Deactivate at any time. This stops all future automatic syncs immediately. Manual sync from the Home screen remains available. Your local data and Excel exports are unaffected.
  • Revoke Google access — You can revoke WinEntry's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. Revoking access will disable Google Sign-In (you will be signed out) and prevent registration record writes. Note: because routine inventory sync runs via a secure server-side process and does not use your personal Google credentials, revoking Google access alone does not stop ongoing background sync. To fully stop sync, also disable cloud backup from Settings → Drive Backup card → Deactivate, or delete your account.
  • Sign out — Signing out from within the app removes all locally cached credentials and sheet IDs from your device.
  • Update registration details — Your registered business name, owner name, phone, and location can be updated at any time from Settings → Business Info.

Inventory sync is handled by a secure server-side process that runs on behalf of the administrator. This process can only access administrator-managed workspace spreadsheets — it has no access to your personal Google Drive files, Gmail, Calendar, or any other Google service.
