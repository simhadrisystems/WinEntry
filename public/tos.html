5. Google Account Sign-In — Scope and Purpose

The App uses Google Sign-In exclusively to verify your identity via Firebase Authentication. The only information requested from your Google account is your email address and a sign-in token (used internally by Firebase to confirm your identity). No other Google account permissions are requested.

The App does not request permission to access Google Sheets, Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service.
  • Account Registration (Settings → Business Info → Save & Register) — Your registration details are submitted through a secure server-side process (Firebase Cloud Function). Your Google credentials are not involved.
  • Account Deletion (Settings → Business Info → ⋮ → Delete Account & Data) — Account and cloud data removal is also handled entirely server-side. Your Google credentials are not involved.
  • Inventory sync — All reads and writes to your cloud workspace are performed server-side by the administrator's Cloud Function using a service account. Your Google credentials are never used for sync.
  • Standard Product List — Fetched from a server-side Cloud Function. Your credentials are not involved.

Your cloud inventory data is stored in standard Google Sheets format in the administrator's managed workspace, which means your data is portable and not locked into a proprietary format.
