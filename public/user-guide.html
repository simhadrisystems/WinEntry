WinEntry — User Guide

Daily Stock Status · Sales · Purchases  |  Simple Inventory for Retailer  |  Updated April 2026

WinEntry ("the App") is an inventory management application designed for retail businesses that manage products as countable units (such as packaged or bottled items), across multiple brands and sizes. This guide covers all features of the app.

1. Installation

WinEntry is distributed as an Android APK file. Your administrator shares the APK via email or another channel.

1Download the file app-arm64-v8a-release.apk (suitable for most modern Android phones) shared by your administrator. 2On your Android phone go to Settings → Security (or Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps on Android 8+) and allow installation from the app you used to receive the file (e.g. your email or file manager). 3Open the downloaded APK and tap Install. 4Once installed, tap Open or find WinEntry in your app drawer.
Requirements: Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later · At least 50 MB free storage.
If you see "App not installed", check that you have enough storage and that your Android version is 8.0+.
The arm64-v8a APK works on the majority of Android phones made after 2017. If it does not install on your device, contact the administrator for the compatible variant.

2. Getting Started

Sign In

WinEntry uses your Google account for identity. Tap Sign In with Google on the login screen and select your account. The app works fully without internet after the first sign-in — you only need connectivity to enable cloud backup features.

First-Time Setup (do this once)

1Business Info — Go to Settings → Business Info. Enter your business name (required). Owner name, phone, and location are optional but useful for report headers. 2Add Products — Go to Products → Product List. Add each brand/product you carry. Each product supports four size variants: QQ, PP, NN, DD. 3Set Display Order — Go to Products → Product Order. Arrange products in the order you want them to appear on the daily stock screen. 4Opening Stock — Go to Products → Opening Stock Setup. Enter the quantities you have on hand on your first trading day. This is the starting point for all sales calculations.
New to the app? Try loading sample data first. Go to Settings → Help & Support → Import Test Data to load 7 days of sample opening balances and closing stock. This lets you explore the app before entering your real data.

4. Daily Stock

This is the core daily workflow. For each product, the app tracks:

Opening Balance → + Purchases → − Closing Balance = Daily Sale

Selecting a Date

The toolbar shows the current date (e.g. 10-MAR). Use the ‹ and › arrows to move between dates, or tap the date itself to open a date picker. You can view or edit data up to 7 days into the future.

Two Modes

ModeWhat you can do
View Mode (default)See all stock figures — opening, purchases, closing, sales. No editing.
CB Entry ModeEdit closing balances for each product and size. Tap CB Entry in the toolbar to switch. Tap View Only to go back.

Entering Closing Balances

1Tap CB Entry to enter edit mode. 2The product list appears. Each product card shows QQ, PP, NN, DD columns with the Opening Balance and Purchases already filled in. 3Tap a closing balance field and enter the quantity counted at end of day. 4The app calculates: Sale = Opening + Purchases − Closing. 5Tap the Save button (green checkmark in toolbar) to commit all changes.
Navigating quickly: The keyboard's Next key moves focus automatically from one size field to the next, and from the last size of one product to the first size of the next product. The list scrolls to keep the active field visible.
Negative sale warning: If your closing balance is higher than opening + purchases (meaning more stock than possible), the app shows a warning. Review the figures before saving.

Day-End Reconciliation (bottom of the list)

After entering closing balances, scroll to the bottom to complete the day-end summary:

  • Total Day Sales — calculated automatically from all products
  • UPI Receipts — enter total UPI/card collections for the day
  • Day Expenses — enter any cash expenses paid
  • Cash for Deposit — calculated as Sales − UPI − Expenses (shown in red if negative)
  • Notes — any remarks for the day

Tap Save at the bottom of the reconciliation section to record the day summary.

Search

Tap the search icon in the toolbar to filter products by name or brand code. The search bar collapses when you tap the back button or press Done on the keyboard.

Unsaved Changes

If you try to leave the screen, change dates, or switch modes with unsaved changes, the app will ask: Save Now / Discard / Keep Editing. Your data is safe until you explicitly discard it.

5. Purchases

Record every supplier delivery here. Purchases are automatically included in the daily stock calculation for the purchase date.

Recording a New Purchase

1Go to Home → Purchases. 2Tap the + button (bottom-right). 3Select the product from the dropdown. 4Enter the purchase date, supplier name, and invoice number. 5For each size (QQ, PP, NN, DD), enter Boxes and Loose units and the unit price. Total units and cost are calculated automatically. 6Tap Save. The purchase is recorded and reflected immediately in the daily stock for that date.
Adding multiple items from the same invoice: In the purchase list, long-press an existing purchase and select Insert Here. This opens a new purchase entry pre-filled with the same invoice number, date, and supplier — just change the product.

Editing or Deleting a Purchase

Long-press any purchase in the list (or tap it) to open an action dialog with options to Edit or Delete. Deletions are permanent and immediately update the daily stock totals.

Searching Purchases

The search bar at the top filters by product name, product code, date, or invoice number. Date formats accepted: dd/MM/yyyy, yyyy-MM-dd, or partial match.

7. Products

Go to Home → Products to manage all product-related settings.

Product List

Add, edit, or remove products. Each product has:

  • Brand code (primary identifier, e.g. W1249) and optional alias codes for import matching
  • Product name displayed in the app
  • Size variants — QQ, PP, NN, DD (enable only the sizes you carry)
  • Purchase price and sale price per unit for each size
  • Units per box for each size (used to convert boxes → loose units)
  • Active / Inactive status — inactive products are hidden from daily stock entry but their history is preserved

Download Standard Product List

If you are registered, you can download a pre-configured product list maintained by the administrator. Go to Products → Download Products.

The standard list is a reference starting point — review all prices and sizes before using for live trading. Your edits and saved records are the authoritative data.

Product Order

Control the order in which products appear on the daily stock screen.

  • Toggle the Edit switch to enter edit mode.
  • Long-press and drag any product to reorder it.
  • Alternatively, type a number in the order badge and tap Renumber & Save.
  • Long-press a product to Deactivate it (moves it to the Inactive section at the bottom). Long-press an inactive product to Re-activate it.
  • Tap Renumber & Save when done, or Discard to cancel changes.

Opening Stock Setup

Enter the quantities you hold on your first trading day. This is done once (or corrected if needed).

1Go to Products → Opening Stock Setup. 2If no data exists, select your first trading day and tap Begin Entry. 3Enter quantities in the grid (QQ, PP, NN, DD per product). Use the keyboard Next key to move between fields. 4Tap SAVE at the bottom. A confirmation shows the total units and value before you confirm.
You can also import opening stock from a closing balance Excel file — use the ⋮ menu → Import from Excel.

The opening stock date is locked once it is set and matches the earliest committed daily stock record. To correct quantities, tap ✎ EDIT, make changes, and save again.

The bottom bar shows sync status and buttons to Upload to Cloud or Restore from Cloud.

9. Cloud Backup

Cloud backup is optional. The app works fully offline without it. Enabling cloud backup stores your inventory data in a private Google Sheet managed by the administrator.

Your data, your control. Signing in with Google does not start any cloud sync. You must explicitly enable backup and accept the terms before any data leaves your device.

How to Enable Cloud Backup

1Register — Go to Settings → Business Info. Fill in your business name and tap Save & Register. This sends your details to the admin. 2Request workspace — Go to Settings → Drive Backup and tap Request Drive Backup. Read and accept the consent terms, then tap I Agree — Enable Backup. 3Workspace created — The app contacts the administrator's system. If your registration is approved, a private workspace sheet is created automatically and cloud backup activates immediately. 4If pending — The Drive Backup card shows "Pending". The admin reviews new registrations and will activate your workspace. Check back after a short wait.

How Sync Works

  • Once active, the app syncs automatically every 6 hours in the background.
  • To sync immediately, tap the Sync button on the Home screen.
  • To stop automatic sync (manual-only), go to Settings → Drive Backup → Deactivate. Manual sync from the Home screen remains available.

What Is Synced

Data typeSynced to cloud
Daily stock entries✓ Yes
Purchase records✓ Yes
Day-end reconciliation✓ Yes
Opening stock✓ Yes
Product listRead-only download from admin
Local business info, GST, address✗ Never (stays on device)
The administrator has read access to all records synced to your workspace. This is the basis on which the free cloud workspace is provided. Do not enable backup if you do not wish the admin to see your records.

Viewer vs Editor Role

The administrator assigns each account one of two roles. Roles are set in Firestore and applied automatically when you sign in.

RoleWhat you can do
Editor (default)Full access — enter and edit data, trigger sync up to cloud and down from cloud, all buttons active
ViewerRead-only access — sync data down from cloud only; data entry, save, and destructive actions are hidden

Most users are Editors. Contact the administrator if you need to change your role.

Revoke or Delete

  • Deactivate — Stops future automatic syncs. Previously synced data remains in the cloud sheet until you delete your account.
  • Delete Account & Data — Permanently removes all cloud data, your registration, and your sign-in account. Go to Settings → Business Info → ⋮ → Delete Account & Data.

After any import, the app shows a result dialog with:

Import Test Data

Available only on fresh installs with no existing daily stock data. Loads a sample set of opening balances and 7 days of closing stock so you can explore the app before entering your real data.

  2. Select the date you want as your sample opening balance day.

Replace sample data with your real figures when you are ready to begin live trading.
